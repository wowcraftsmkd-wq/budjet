# ചായ ☕ വൈബ് — Treatment

Style: **Watercolor Brush** (`styles/watercolor`). Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 56.7 s (stretched from ~42 s so every Malayalam line stays on screen long enough to read).
Language: Malayalam on screen and in the voice-over (male neural voice ml-IN-MidhunNeural, one line
per on-screen line, music ducked ~4 dB under it). A first cut without voice is kept as chaya-vibe.mp4. Ends on a call to action: "comment your chai spot".

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **One glass, one life.** The same tea glass on the same wooden counter is the anchor; its steam
   rises and every puff of steam opens a memory (rain, the pour, the snack cabinet, the radio bench,
   the night stall). We return to the glass at the end. A loop that bookends.
2. **A day at a chaayakkada.** Dawn to night on one locked shop front, time painting over itself
   (morning bus, noon rain, evening radio, night bulbs). Calm, but slow for a reel and the hook is weak.
3. **A price list that turns.** A hand-written menu board: "ചായ ₹10", and each item on the list
   ("മഴ", "പത", "പഴംപൊരി", "കൂട്ടുകാർ") is priced "?" and painted. Clever but reads as an ad.

**Chosen: 1.** The approved script opens on the price of a glass and ends on "the vibe"; the glass
is the obvious hero, and steam is the medium's own transition (the style's *mist erase*, made of
tea steam). It also gives the reel a loop: the last frame sits close to the first, which helps replays.

## 2. Logline and arc

A ten-rupee glass of tea turns out to hold everything that mattered: rain, friends, first salary,
the worst night, so we don't go for the tea, we go for the vibe.

- Setup (0–6.7 s): the price question; the glass on the counter.
- Build (6.7–26.7 s): four memories, each opened by steam: rain, the pull, the cabinet, the bench.
- Turn / peak (26.7–33.3 s): the night stall with friends: joy and heartbreak, the one emotional line.
- Ending (33.3–42 s): near-silence, back to the single glass: "ആ വൈബിന് വേണ്ടി." Then the CTA.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Studio food-animation close-ups** (the hand-drawn cooking/eating scenes of Japanese feature
  animation). Learn: linger on steam and texture, slow loving inserts, food as memory.
  Don't take: any character, design, shot or colour script.
- **Urban-sketcher travel journals of Kerala streets** (pen-and-wash sketchbooks). Learn: the
  shop-front grammar, banana bunches, thatched eaves, hand-lettered notes beside the subject.
  Don't take: any specific drawing or layout.

## 8. Type

- Main lines: **Noto Serif Malayalam** (OFL), ink at ~85 % over a paper halo, centred in the
  lower third / upper area depending on subject, ≤ 2 lines, revealed left-to-right like writing.
- Hand notes on specimens (snack names, "₹10?"): **Chilanka** (OFL Malayalam handwriting).
- Big payoff "ആ വൈബിന് വേണ്ടി." and CTA: Noto Serif Malayalam Bold, large.
- One accent colour: **vermilion `#c8442a`** reserved for the tea glass's rim highlight / the
  CTA arrow and the "?" marks.
- No subtitles box; the lines *are* the subtitles (burned in; `subtitles_ml.srt` exported).
